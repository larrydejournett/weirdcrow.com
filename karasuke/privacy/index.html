Privacy policy

Karasuke, by Weird Crow · Effective November 18, 2026

The short version: Karasuke does not collect, store or share any personal data. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

What the game stores

Karasuke saves your settings (music, sound, tutorial) and your best scores on your own device. This information never leaves your device, and we can't see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Game Center

If you choose to sign in to Apple's Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple to show on leaderboards. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We don't receive your name, email or other account details.

What we don't do

The game has no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no accounts, no in-app purchases, and no links to outside websites. It doesn't use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Children

Because the game collects no personal data, it collects none from children.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. These help us fix bugs and don't identify you.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
