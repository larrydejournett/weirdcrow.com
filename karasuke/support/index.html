Common questions

How do I play?

On iPhone and iPad, drag to fly and tap to drop. On Mac, use the arrow keys to fly and Space to drop. On Apple TV, swipe and hold on the Siri Remote to fly and click to drop. A game controller works on all devices. Replay the tutorial any time with the TUTORIAL button on the title screen.

How do I grow bigger?

Eat bread. Each size makes your droppings worth more and lets you flatten bigger things. Getting hit, or going too long without eating, shrinks you again.

How do I beat a boss?

Each boss has a weak moment. The newspaper before every fight gives you a hint, and the boss's health bar at the top shows your progress.

My scores or progress disappeared.

Your best scores are saved on your device. Deleting the app removes them. Leaderboard scores and achievements are kept by Game Center and come back when you reinstall and sign in.

Do I need Game Center?

No. The game plays the same without it. If you sign in to Game Center later, achievements you earned are sent then.

The music sounds odd on AirPods.

Spatial Audio can make the music sound narrow. Turn it off in Control Center while playing.

Does the game have ads or purchases?

No. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no account to create.
